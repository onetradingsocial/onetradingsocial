## Show HN, when you are ready

The rule is that people must be able to try the thing without signing up. The
three calculators qualify; the product does not.

**Title (80 max), pick one:**

- `Show HN: Three free trading calculators – position size, expectancy, prop-firm rules`
- `Show HN: Position size, expectancy and prop-firm consistency calculators`

**Body:**

> I build a trading journal, and kept needing these three sums while writing about them, so I put them on their own pages. No sign-up, nothing stored, no analytics on the inputs — the arithmetic runs in the browser and the page never sends what you type anywhere.
>
> - Position size: account size, risk %, entry and stop → the size that makes the stop cost exactly your risk.
> - Expectancy: win rate, average win and average loss → what one trade is worth, the break-even win rate, and profit factor.
> - Prop-firm consistency: profit so far, best day, the firm's percentage → whether you are inside the rule, the largest day you can still take, and how much more profit brings an oversized day back inside it.
>
> Two decisions I would defend: none of them suggests a number — no "risk 1–2%", no target expectancy — because the right number depends on things a calculator cannot see. And the prop-firm examples are quoted from each firm's own rules page with the date I checked, since those terms change.
>
> Happy to be told the maths is wrong. It is three pages of vanilla JS, no framework.

**First comment, straight after posting:** the context — pre-revenue, founder-only,
what the journal does, and that the calculators came out of the posts rather
than the other way round.

   from the page, so check both and replace them with the **short description**

4. Fill in: pricing **Freemium, from A$30/month, free trial**; categories
