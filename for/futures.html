Questions

Futures trading journal, answered

What's the best futures trading journal for ES and NQ?

TradingSocial is built for index futures (ES, NQ, YM, RTY), micros (MES, MNQ) and commodities (CL, GC). Log contract size and tick value with every trade, tag the session and setup, and see your R multiples and consistency score in one dashboard, not a spreadsheet.

Does TradingSocial sync with my futures broker automatically?

No. There is no CME, CQG or Rithmic-style futures broker sync. MT5 is the only automatic broker sync TradingSocial offers today, so futures trades, ES, NQ, CL, GC and the rest, are logged manually: enter the contract, size, entry and exit, and tick value calculates your R automatically.

Can I journal micro futures like MES and MNQ?

Yes. Micro contracts like MES and MNQ log the same way as their full-size counterparts. Set the contract and tick value once, and every trade's R multiple scales correctly, whether you're trading one micro or ten.

How does R multiple work across ES, NQ, GC and CL with different tick values?

Enter the contract and its tick value when you log a trade, and TradingSocial calculates R consistently whether you're trading ES, GC, CL or a micro. That keeps your risk-adjusted stats comparable across a mixed futures book.

How is a futures trading leaderboard ranked fairly?

The leaderboard ranks risk-adjusted return and consistency, not raw profit and loss, so one lucky trade in NQ doesn't outrank a trader compounding small edges every session. Only Trader and Pro plan members are ranked.
