Questions

Which page is right for you?

Which trading journal should I use for my market?

Any of them work the same way underneath: manual logging, R-based risk, weekly reviews and a public profile. Pick the page for your market (forex, crypto or futures) to see it described in your terms, or start from Trading journal for the general view. The underlying journal is identical.

Can I journal more than one market in the same account?

Yes. Trades are tagged by symbol and market, not siloed into separate accounts, so a forex swing, a crypto perpetual and a futures contract can all sit in the same journal. Your dashboard, weekly review and leaderboard placement cover everything you log, whichever market it came from.

Do I need a broker connection to use a trading journal?

No. Every feature works from manual entries alone: setup, size, entry, exit and risk in R, logged by you. MT5 remains the only broker with automatic sync; on Pro, Binance also syncs automatically for crypto. Neither has ever been required to journal properly.

Is there a free trading journal I can start with?

Yes. The Free plan logs trades, tags and check-ins with no card required, keeping your last 30 trades visible at any time. Trader and Pro add unlimited history, weekly reviews, MT5 import and automatic sync. Pro also includes a 14-day free trial before any card is charged.
